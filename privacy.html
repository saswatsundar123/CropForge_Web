privacy_policy

No tracking. No data collection.

// Effective date: 2026 · MIT Project

CropForge.org is a static documentation and project website for the open-source CropForge Python library. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information.

What we collect: Nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no session identifiers, and no third-party scripts that collect user data.

GitHub API: The repository statistics section fetches public data from api.github.com. This request is made client-side from your browser to GitHub's servers. GitHub's privacy policy applies to that request.

External links: Clicking links to GitHub, ReadTheDocs, or PyPI takes you to third-party sites governed by their own privacy policies.

Fonts: All fonts (Google Sans Code) are self-hosted and served directly from this site. No Google Fonts CDN requests are made.

Contact: For privacy-related concerns, open an issue at github.com/saswatsundar123/cropforge/issues.
